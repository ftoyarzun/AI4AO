Searching for a Better CNN

AI4AO · CNN-comparison instrument (unmodulated Pyramid at 700 nm, 190 modes, four 42×42 pupils, noise on) · run 2026-10-05 · params file CNNComparison_params.py, SHA-256 D1E32B7F…FEDB9B4ED, unchanged throughout

Main finding

The training recipe, not the architecture, carries the gain.

A higher learning rate with warm-up and cosine decay, plus a 5× learning rate on the stem, improves BestCNN by 10.1 nm RMS in steady-state closed loop (173.1 → 163.0 nm; oracle floor 140.6 nm). Under that same recipe, the best redesigned CNN, a 1.0M-parameter variant, is 1.8 ± 0.3 nm worse than BestCNN across three training seeds. BestCNN remains the best architecture, and the 1.0M variant is a compact option that costs 1.8 nm.

Findings

Measured

  • The baseline moved with the physics. Commit 100905f made the moving turbulence √2 stronger. On evaluation seed 1234, the one BestCNN.ipynb uses, the original checkpoint gives 117 nm on the code just before that commit and 182 nm after it (166.2 nm averaged over five seeds); its recorded ≈106 nm is about 11 nm lower still, for a reason not traced here. The oracle floor is now 140.6 nm. Numbers recorded before 2026-09-29 are not comparable.
  • BestCNN's stem never trained at lr 1e-4: its first layer ended indistinguishable from its random initialization, with update ratios 4× below the deep layers.
  • The deep stages are mostly unused capacity. Cutting them from 2.95M to 1.0M parameters cost nothing measurable in screening; after training they keep 94–99% of their initial effective rank. A uniform shrink of every layer cost about 6 nm.
  • ModalMAE is the right loss. RMSELoss was +7.9 ± 2.3 nm, the hybrid +4.4 ± 2.4, log-variance a tie (−0.5 ± 0.9).
  • Training-seed noise is 0.0–0.3 nm under the tuned recipe, not the assumed 5–10 nm. Spread across evaluation seeds (~20 nm) is atmosphere.

Interpretation and limits

  • The redesign (cross-pupil front, ceil pooling, spatial head) looked like a 7.5 nm win in screening because the candidates were tuned and BestCNN was not. Retraining BestCNN under the same recipe removed the win. Lesson: retrain the baseline whenever the candidate gets extra tuning.
  • Relative modal error on fresh batches does not predict closed-loop rank across losses. Decide on closed-loop nm RMS.
  • The integrator filters reconstruction error: a 119 vs 157 nm per-frame modal error became a 9 nm steady-state difference.
  • The 1.0M model is not faster on this GPU; batch-16 training and inference are launch-bound.
  • Untested: longer training, other warm-up fractions or peak learning rates, weight decay, batch size, other instruments.
